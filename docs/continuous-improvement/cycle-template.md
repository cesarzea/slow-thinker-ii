## Context and hypothesis

Describe delivery scope, starting point, improvement hypotheses and the proposal
identifiers under evaluation. Identify approved decisions and pending proposals.

## Measurement basis

Record start/end observations, pauses and their evidence sources. Distinguish
active elapsed time from accumulated participant activity. Partition intervals
without double counting within a participant. Separate measured values, estimates,
lower bounds and reliably unattributed time. Link structured evidence and provenance.

## Delivery and process results

Record verified behavior and evidence, adherence to the method and deviations,
rework/defects, repeated checks and their causes. Distinguish product defects from
fixture, environment, expectation and verification-command errors.

## Evaluation and limitations

Explain what the evidence supports and what cannot be concluded. Compare prior
cycles with scope and starting-point differences explicit. Do not infer causal
productivity gains from test counts, code volume or parallel participant count.

## Next-cycle decision

Separate proposals from approved decisions. Link their identifiers, decision owner
and date; retain a new method version if the procedure changes. Append dated
corrections rather than overwriting historical measurements or conclusions.
