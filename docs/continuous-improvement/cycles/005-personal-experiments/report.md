## Evaluation and limitations

The phases and exclusive assignments were applied, but representative checks were
too narrow: numeric authoring did not establish the saved-run canvas path, and
isolated test sets missed complete collection/dead-code integration issues.
Clarifications and review corrections demonstrate remaining preparation gaps.
They do not quantify duplicated analysis or its cost.

This larger product sprint differs from C03's presentation changes and C04's
maintenance. There is no reconciled time/resource basis for a causal efficiency
comparison. Parallelism, test counts and fewer questions alone cannot prove savings.
The evidence supports concrete preparation and acceptance weaknesses, not a claim
that M06 is faster or slower overall.
